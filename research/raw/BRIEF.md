# Shared brief for all research agents

## Who this is for
Lumen adl is a small digital studio in Adelaide, South Australia (websites, SEO). The owner, Troy, wants to move into
HIGH-TICKET AI automation builds. He met the owner of a local Adelaide real estate agency who wants AI in the business.
We need automation ideas that SAVE the agency money or MAKE it money, with emphasis on UNIQUE, NICHE ideas few
people are doing. Obvious ones (missed-call text back, instant enquiry reply, generic chatbot, listing copy via ChatGPT,
generic CRM follow-up) are known: Lumen already sells instant enquiry replies. Mention obvious ones only briefly.
We want the clever, specific, under-served plays, ideally with proof someone is doing it and getting results.

## Australian context (important)
Australian agencies differ from US realtors: sales agents + a property management (PM) department (the "rent roll"
is a valuable asset, PM fees are recurring revenue). Portals are realestate.com.au (REA) and Domain, not Zillow/MLS.
Common software: AgentBox, Rex, VaultRE, Box+Dice, MRI/Console Cloud, PropertyMe, PropertyTree, Ailo, :Different,
inspection apps (Inspection Express, Kolmeo, Tapi for maintenance), 2Apply/Snug/Ignite for rental applications,
Rental bond via CBS in SA. Auctions are common in Adelaide. SA has the Form 1 (vendor's statement), cooling-off,
Residential Tenancies Act SA, and from 1 July 2026 real estate agents fall under AML/CTF "Tranche 2" obligations.
Spam Act, Do Not Call Register and Privacy Act apply. US ideas are fine to report, but note how they translate to SA.

## Output
Write your findings to your assigned output file (markdown). Use this structure for each idea (aim for 8-15 ideas,
quality over quantity, unique ones first):

### <Idea name>
- **What it does:** 2-3 sentences, concrete workflow (trigger -> AI step -> output).
- **Money:** saves or makes money how; any numbers found (hours saved, $ value, conversion lift). Mark estimates as estimates.
- **How rare:** common / emerging / rare, with why you think so.
- **Evidence:** who is doing it or talking about it, with source URLs (Reddit threads, posts, videos, case studies, vendors).
- **SA fit:** how it fits an Adelaide agency (software, laws, market quirks). Any compliance risk.
- **Build notes:** likely stack (n8n/Make, Claude/OpenAI, Twilio, Retell/Vapi, CRM APIs) and difficulty (low/med/high).

End the file with a "## Best 3 from my angle" section ranking the 3 strongest for a high-ticket pitch, and a
"## Notable quotes" section with 3-8 verbatim pain-point quotes from agents/PMs (with source URL).

## Rules
- Read TOOLS.md in the same folder first and use those commands (Agent Reach: Exa, Reddit via Arctic Shift, YouTube search).
- Cite real URLs you actually saw. Never invent sources, numbers or companies. If unverified, say so.
- Keep moving: ~25-40 tool calls is plenty. Don't write files other than your output file.
- Your final reply to me: 5-10 line summary of your best findings (the file holds the detail).
